Guillaume Blanc

I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at Simon Fraser University. My research explores how culture and institutions shape long-run development. My work has been featured in media outlets such as The Atlantic, The Guardian, El Mundo, and Vox, and I am a recipient of an Emergent Ventures grant.

Upcoming visits: Pitt (April 20), Penn (April 22), and Northwestern (April 23).
